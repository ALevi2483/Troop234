Important Information

Camp Dates and Cost Are Still Being Finalized

Troop 234 is still reviewing summer camp dates and costs. Our attendance dates and the cost to attend have not yet been confirmed. We will share more information here as it becomes available.

Please do not register individually for camp. Troop 234 will register as a troop. We will share instructions for confirming your Scout’s participation when they are available.

Our Troop Advancement Coordinator will coordinate all merit badge and event sign-ups. Review the program information to identify your interests and wait for the troop’s sign-up instructions.

Before Camp

  • Read the parent guide and review troop camp updates.
  • Do not register individually. We will register as a troop and provide participation instructions when available.
  • Review merit badge and event options, including any listed prerequisites. Our Troop Advancement Coordinator will coordinate all merit badge and event sign-ups.
  • If you have not completed Medical Form Part C with your doctor, we recommend doing so sooner rather than later. Download the medical forms packet.
  • Review the camp’s packing instructions.
  • Watch for troop updates on departure, transportation, and pickup arrangements.

Questions? Contact troop leadership at a meeting or through the troop’s Slack.
