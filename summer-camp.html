Do Not Register for Summer Camp Individually

Please do not register your Scout directly through Camp Raven Knob or sign up for merit badges and programs on your own.

Troop 234’s Summer Camp Coordinator will register our Scouts and leaders as a troop. This includes coordinating our camp registration and collecting the information needed for merit badge and program selections.

  • Review the 2027 Program Guide and Program Grid.
  • Look through the available merit badges, programs, and activities.
  • Decide which programs and merit badges your Scout is interested in.
  • Keep those selections available so you are ready when Troop 234 requests your Scout’s choices.
  • Wait for instructions from the troop before submitting registrations, payments, merit badge selections, or program selections.

Reviewing the Raven Knob website and program materials is encouraged. We want Scouts to know what they would like to do. Please use those materials for planning only and do not complete an individual registration.

Troop 234 will let families know when it is time to submit Scout information, program choices, merit badge selections, forms, and payments.

2027 Camp Deadlines & Key Dates

The dates below come from Camp Raven Knob’s 2027 camp information. Troop 234 may establish earlier internal deadlines so we have enough time to collect forms, payments, and program selections.

Parents and Scouts: These dates are provided for planning purposes. Do not register or make individual camp payments unless Troop 234 specifically instructs you to do so. The troop will coordinate our unit registration.

Registration and Planning Dates

September 1, 2026
2027 campsite assignments begin

Camp management begins assigning 2027 campsites based on unit reservations and campsite preferences.

Before February 1, 2027
Unit deposit withdrawal deadline

A unit withdrawing before February 1 may qualify for the partial unit-deposit refund described in the Leader Guide.

February 2027
Pre-camp webinars begin

Camp Raven Knob begins monthly pre-camp webinars covering registration, programs, and camp preparation.

February 15, 2027
Unit Count opens

Units begin submitting Scout and leader counts. Camp requires the Unit Count payment before session registration can be finalized.

March 16, 2027
Session registration opens for Weeks 1 and 2

Merit badge and program session registration opens for units attending Weeks 1 and 2.

March 18, 2027
Session registration opens for Weeks 3 through 6

Merit badge and program session registration opens for units attending Weeks 3, 4, 5, and 6.

April 15, 2027
Unit Count locks

This is the last day units can directly change their Unit Count online. Changes after this date must be coordinated with camp administration.

April or May 2027
Sunday arrival-time signup

Units will select a Sunday arrival appointment. Normal arrival appointments are between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM.

May 15, 2027
Transportation request deadline

Units needing Raven Knob transportation from designated airport or train locations must submit their request by this date.

May 31 / June 1, 2027
Camp contact location changes

Camp administrative operations transition from the Old Hickory Council office to Camp Raven Knob for the summer season.

Before Arrival
Final camp balance

The unit balance must be paid before arrival to qualify for Express Check-In. Any remaining balance will be collected during camp check-in.

Before September 1, 2027
2028 campsite preference

Units attending in 2027 should submit their next-year deposit before September 1 to retain preference for their same campsite and week for 2028.

Important Dates and Times During Camp

Monday After Lunch: Fishing Permits

Scouts and leaders who want to fish at Camp Raven Knob must obtain a Raven Knob Fishing Permit. Fishing permits are issued Monday after lunch following a brief fishing orientation and safety session. Fishing is catch-and-release.

Day Time Activity or Deadline
Sunday 1:00 PM to 3:30 PM Normal troop arrival appointments. Early arrival must be coordinated with camp.
Sunday During check-in Unit photographs are taken during the check-in process.
Sunday About 5:50 PM Evening assembly.
Sunday 6:00 PM Supper followed by the unit leader meeting.
Monday 8:15 AM Day Campers check in.
Monday to Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Normal Day Camper program hours.
Monday Breakfast Individual Scout schedules and CRK Passports are placed in unit mailboxes.
Monday After lunch Fishing orientation and Raven Knob Fishing Permit issuance.
Monday Begins Monday Big Stepper competition begins for adult leaders.
Monday Recommended mailing cutoff Camp recommends that letters or packages not be mailed later than Monday of the Scout’s camp week because delivery may take several days.
Wednesday 5:30 PM Visitors' Night begins. Visitors may arrive beginning at 5:30 PM. Units eat supper in their campsites, followed by the campfire and OA ceremony.
Thursday Twilight Scouts participating in the Climbing Experience must attend Open Climbing Twilight.
Thursday After supper Adult Leader Social and Scoutmaster Cook-off on the parade ground.
Friday 12:00 PM Big Stepper counting period ends.
Friday By 5:00 PM Big Stepper totals must be submitted to Administration.
Friday Evening Closing campfire. Units receive checkout materials, advancement reports, and health forms afterward.
Saturday 7:30 AM Continental breakfast and flag assembly.
Saturday 8:00 AM Cleanup, packing, and departures begin.
Saturday By 10:30 AM All units must be out of camp.

RAMPAGE Program Schedule

Scouts participating in the RAMPAGE high-adventure program follow a separate schedule during the week.

Day RAMPAGE Schedule
Sunday 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM gear drop-off at Scoutcraft and swim check with the troop.
Sunday Meet at Scoutcraft at 4:00 PM with all RAMPAGE gear. Participants eat supper at Raven Knob and then travel to the RAMPAGE base in Independence, Virginia.
Monday Whitewater canoeing on the New River, lunch on the river, and overnight at the RAMPAGE base.
Tuesday River boarding at New River Trail State Park, followed by return to Camp Raven Knob and overnight with the troop.
Wednesday Hike Raven Knob after breakfast, climbing at Pilot Mountain, return for Visitors' Night, supper and campfire, then return to Independence.
Thursday Travel to Grayson Highlands State Park and begin the overnight backpacking portion of the program.
Friday Complete the backpacking trip, clean up at the RAMPAGE base, return to Camp Raven Knob for supper, and attend the closing campfire.

RAMPAGE check-in note: The dedicated RAMPAGE information lists the Sunday meeting time as 4:00 PM. The 2027 Leader Guide lists RAMPAGE and Cripple Creek participants as meeting at Scoutcraft by 4:30 PM.

Troop 234 will use 4:00 PM for planning unless Camp Raven Knob provides updated instructions.

Before Camp

  • Read the parent guide and review the 2027 camp deadlines and key dates .
  • Do not register individually through Camp Raven Knob. Troop 234’s Summer Camp Coordinator will register Scouts and leaders as a troop.
  • Review merit badge, program, and activity options. Make a list of the choices your Scout is interested in and keep that information available.
  • Do not submit merit badge or program selections directly to Camp Raven Knob. Wait for Troop 234 to request your Scout’s selections.
  • If you have not completed Medical Form Part C with your doctor, we recommend doing so sooner rather than later. Download the medical forms packet.
  • Review the camp’s packing instructions.
  • If your Scout plans to fish, remember that Raven Knob Fishing Permits are issued Monday after lunch following the required orientation and safety session.
  • Watch for Troop 234 updates on our selected camp week, cost, payment schedule, forms, program selections, departure, transportation, and pickup arrangements.

Questions? Contact troop leadership at a meeting or through the troop’s Slack.
