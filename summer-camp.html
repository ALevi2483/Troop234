Before Camp

  • Read the parent guide and review troop camp updates.
  • Confirm registration, payment, and paperwork deadlines.
  • Review program options and any listed prerequisites.
  • Confirm medical paperwork requirements with troop leadership.
  • Review the camp’s packing instructions.
  • Confirm departure, transportation, and pickup arrangements.

Questions? Contact troop leadership at a meeting or through the troop’s Slack.
