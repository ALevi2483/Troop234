⚠️ Do Not Register Individually

Please do not register your Scout directly through Camp Raven Knob or submit merit badge and program selections on your own.

Troop 234's Summer Camp Coordinator will register Scouts and leaders as a troop.

1. Review Look through the programs and merit badges.
2. Choose Decide what your Scout is interested in doing.
3. Wait Have those choices ready when Troop 234 asks for them.

You are encouraged to review all of the Raven Knob program information. Please use it for planning only until Troop 234 provides registration instructions.

Troop 234 will tell families when it is time to submit selections, forms, and payments.

What You Should Do Now

You do not need to register yet. Focus on these four things.

1

Review the Program Guide

Look through the merit badges, special programs, activities, age recommendations, prerequisites, and additional program fees.

2

Make a List of Your Scout's Choices

Have preferred merit badges and programs ready when Troop 234 requests selections.

3

Complete Medical Form Part C

If your Scout needs a physical, schedule it early. Parts A, B, and C are required for camp.

4

Watch for Troop 234 Instructions

We will provide the camp week, final cost, payment schedule, form deadline, and instructions for submitting program choices.

Additional Camp Details

These details are available for anyone who wants them, but most families do not need to act on them directly.

2027 Camp Weeks
  • Week 1: June 20 to June 26
  • Week 2: June 27 to July 3
  • Week 3: July 4 to July 10
  • Week 4: July 11 to July 17
  • Week 5: July 18 to July 24
  • Week 6: July 25 to July 31
Camp Registration Dates

These are handled by Troop 234's Summer Camp Coordinator. Families should not register individually.

  • February 15: Unit Count registration opens.
  • March 16: Merit badge and session registration opens for Weeks 1 and 2.
  • March 18: Merit badge and session registration opens for Weeks 3 through 6.
  • April 15: Unit Count locks.
  • One week before arrival: Raven Knob's session registration portal closes.
Camp Arrival & Departure
  • Raven Knob Sunday check-in appointments are between 1:00 PM and 3:30 PM.
  • Troop 234's actual departure time from Chesapeake will be announced separately.
  • Saturday continental breakfast begins at 7:30 AM.
  • Packing and departure begin around 8:00 AM.
  • All units must leave Camp Raven Knob before 10:30 AM Saturday.
  • Troop 234's estimated return time to Chesapeake will be announced later.
Adult Leader Pricing
  • Full-week adult leader: $240
  • Half-week adult leader: $150
  • Raven Knob waives one full-week adult leader fee for every 10 Scouts attending with the unit.
Express Health Check-In

Raven Knob offers an expedited Sunday health check when the unit sends paper copies of the required medical and release forms to camp at least two weeks before arrival.

Troop 234 will provide families with our own form collection deadline so the troop has enough time to review and submit the paperwork.
